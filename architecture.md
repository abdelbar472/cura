each of these agents will be implemented as a separate module, allowing for modular development and testing. The orchestrator will manage the flow of information between the different agents, ensuring that each agent receives the necessary input and produces the required output. The symptom analyzer will process patient-reported symptoms and generate a list of potential conditions. The lab interpreter will analyze laboratory results and provide insights into possible diagnoses. The differential diagnosis agent will compare symptoms and lab results to generate a prioritized list of potential conditions. The drug interaction agent will check for potential interactions between prescribed medications. Finally, the imaging analyzer will process medical images, starting with X-rays and later expanding to include CT angiography (CTA) scans.
